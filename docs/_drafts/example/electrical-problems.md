# Exposed wires, blown fuse or other electrical problems

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 8, Electrical Safety; p. 11, Connecting the Electrical Power · Retrieved 2026-11-12

The power supply sends high current to the laser tube terminals. Electrical faults must be handled by staff and support only.

## Signs

- A silicone terminal cover has slipped and bare wire is visible.
- A fuse has blown.

## Steps

1. Stop using the machine immediately if covers have slipped and bare wire is exposed. {staff_only: true}
2. Staff: notify Full Spectrum Laser support. {staff_only: true}
3. Do not open any access panels while the machine is plugged in. {staff_only: true}
4. Never touch electrical areas with hands or tools unless the machine has been disconnected from power for at least one hour. {staff_only: true}
5. Never make or break electrical connections while the machine is on. {staff_only: true}
6. If a fuse blows, contact Technical Support before replacing anything. Never fit a fuse with a higher rating than the installed 20 amp fuse. {staff_only: true}
