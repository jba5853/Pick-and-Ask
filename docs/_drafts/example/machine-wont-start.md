# Machine won't start

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 12, Power On Machine; p. 8, Electrical Safety; p. 20, Laser Test; p. 24, Control Panel; p. 30, General Maintenance · Retrieved 2026-11-12

The laser will not start unless the ignition key is in the correct position, and the emergency stop button is the control users most often press by accident.

## Signs

- The laser will not start.
- The emergency stop button may have been pressed by accident.

## Steps

1. Make sure no material is near the belts, laser head or moving parts, because the laser head moves to the front right when the machine boots up.
2. Insert the ignition key into the ignition switch.
   REVIEW: Staff may keep the ignition keys. Decide whether visitors may use them.
3. Turn the ignition switch to the on position.
4. With the key in, the laser is started using the emergency stop button. This is the button users most often press without meaning to, so check that it is in the correct position.
   REVIEW: The source does not explain how the emergency stop button starts the laser (for example twisting to release). Staff should clarify.
5. Main power is controlled by the on/off switch at the back of the machine. {staff_only: true}
   REVIEW: The source describes this switch inconsistently: the '(-)' side pressed for on, 'flip the on/off switch up', and a 'red power switch'.
6. Give the machine time to boot up.
7. If it still won't work, press Diagnose on the control panel to display any current errors and show them to staff.
   REVIEW: Showing the errors to staff is added guidance. The source only says what the button does.
8. If a fuse has blown, contact Technical Support before replacing anything. {staff_only: true}
