# Laser won't fire

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 6, Safety During Operation; p. 12, Power On Machine; pp. 16-17, Setup the Water System · Retrieved 2026-11-12
> REVIEW: The source has no 'laser won't fire' section. This doc combines the interlock, key and water flow sensor notes.

The laser is blocked from firing if the lid is open, if the key or start button is not in position, or if the water chiller is not connected correctly.

## Signs

- The job or Pulse button runs but the laser does not fire.
- The lid or door was opened during operation.

## Steps

1. Make sure the lid is closed. Never tamper with the lid safety mechanism. An interlock switches the laser off if the door is opened during operation.
2. Check that the key and the power button are in the correct position.
3. Staff: check that the water chiller is correctly connected, powered and pumping. Never fire the laser without it. {staff_only: true}
4. Staff: check that the chiller tubes are not connected backwards (In to Out, Out to In). The water flow sensor stops the laser firing if they are. {staff_only: true}
