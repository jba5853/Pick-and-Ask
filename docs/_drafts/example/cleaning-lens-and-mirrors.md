# Cleaning the lens and mirrors

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 30, General Maintenance · Retrieved 2026-11-12
> REVIEW: The source does not explain how to reach or remove the mirrors and lens.

Clean the optics weekly, or more often if materials leave heavy residue, using only optical wipes or a microfiber cloth.

## Steps

1. Use only a microfiber cloth or lens wipes. Anything else can micro-scratch the optics. {staff_only: true}
2. Weekly, use optical-grade lens wipes to clean both sides of the beam combiner, all mirrors, the focus lens and the tube aperture. {staff_only: true}
3. Clean more often, possibly daily, if materials produce a lot of residue. {staff_only: true}
