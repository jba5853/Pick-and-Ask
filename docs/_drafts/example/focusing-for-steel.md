# Setting up autofocus for cutting steel

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 23, Focusing · Retrieved 2026-11-12
> REVIEW: The source gives no other steel-cutting guidance such as settings or gas.

Steel cutting needs the 2.5" lens, the Metal switch setting and an autofocus calibration.

## Steps

1. Make sure the 2.5" (63.5mm) lens is installed in the lower slot. {staff_only: true}
2. Set the right-side switch to Metal. {staff_only: true}
3. With the steel under the cutting head, press FOCUS on the side panel. {staff_only: true}
4. The head jogs down until it touches the steel and then returns, which calibrates the autofocus. {staff_only: true}
