# Air bubbles in the laser tube and cooling water care

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, pp. 16-17, Setup the Water System; p. 20, Laser Test; p. 30, General Maintenance (Monthly) · Retrieved 2026-11-12

Air bubbles in the tube's water jacket shorten the tube's life. The cooling water should be distilled and changed regularly.

## Signs

- Air bubbles are visible in the water jacket of the laser tube, often near the electrodes at either end.

## Steps

1. Make sure there are no air bubbles in the water jacket of the laser tube. {staff_only: true}
2. Remove the tube access panel to check for air bubbles. {staff_only: true}
3. Turn on the water chiller and let it pump water through the tube. {staff_only: true}
4. If there are bubbles, squeeze the water inlet pipe and release it to break them free. {staff_only: true}
5. If bubbles persist, drain the tube completely, turn the chiller back on and check again. {staff_only: true}
6. Change the cooling water monthly, using only distilled water. {staff_only: true}
   REVIEW: For cold climates, p. 17 says to use a 20% propylene glycol mix, while p. 30 says 'laser antifreeze'. Confirm which to use.
