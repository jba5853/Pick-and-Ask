# Checks before starting a job

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 30, General Maintenance (Before starting a job, Daily); p. 5, Setup Precautions; p. 6, Safety During Operation; p. 14, Connect the Exhaust System; p. 7, Fire Safety · Retrieved 2026-11-12

Checks to make before each job: inspect the machine, check the material, clear debris, and confirm the exhaust and air assist are working.

## Steps

1. Inspect the laser cutter for damage or breakage before each use. Do not use it if anything is damaged or defective.
2. Check the MSDS of your material and never cut anything containing PVC.
3. Make sure the lens and optics are clean, using only a microfiber cloth or lens wipes. {staff_only: true}
4. Check the gantry rails for debris. If particles are present, wipe them clean with a rag.
   REVIEW: Decide whether visitors may wipe the rails or should only report debris.
5. If debris has fallen into the gantry rails, staff should remove the belt to clear it, because it can interfere with the laser output. {staff_only: true}
6. Make sure no material is near the belts, laser head or other moving parts.
7. Make sure both exhaust fans are on whenever a job is run.
   REVIEW: Decide whether visitors switch the fans on or staff do.
8. Staff: check that the water, air, exhaust and power systems are properly assembled and working normally. {staff_only: true}
9. Always use the air assist, especially when vector cutting.
   REVIEW: The air compressor plugs into the machine and only turns on when the laser fires (p. 18).
10. Keep the area around the machine clear, and never store flammable materials in or near it.
