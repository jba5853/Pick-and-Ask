# Flames while cutting

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 7, Fire Safety; p. 5, Setup Precautions; p. 31, Warranty · Retrieved 2026-11-12
> REVIEW: The source does not say what to do during an active fire. The safety message applies; staff should add the local procedure.

Most cuttable materials can ignite. A small flame at the cut usually goes out on its own thanks to the air assist, but a flame can spread to the machine and its surroundings.

## Signs

- A small flame coming from the cut zone.
- The flame spreads to the material, the machine or the surroundings.

## Steps

1. Never leave the laser unattended while it is running.
2. Always use the air assist, especially when vector cutting, which has the most potential to create an open flame. {staff_only: true}
3. Stand next to the machine and be ready to turn it off in an emergency.
   REVIEW: This guidance comes from the Warranty section. Confirm the local emergency stop and fire procedure.
4. Staff keep a maintained 5 lb or larger fire extinguisher (halogen or multipurpose dry chemical) near the machine. {staff_only: true}
5. Staff remove debris and leftover material from inside the machine to prevent fires. {staff_only: true}
