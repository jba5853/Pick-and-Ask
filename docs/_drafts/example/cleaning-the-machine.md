# Cleaning debris from the machine

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 30, General Maintenance (Weekly); p. 5, Setup Precautions · Retrieved 2026-11-12

Built-up residue and debris is a fire hazard and hampers the exhaust, so clean the machine regularly.

## Signs

- Debris or small pieces have built up in the tray or under the cutting grid.

## Steps

1. Wipe down the walls of the machine with a clean rag. {staff_only: true}
   REVIEW: Decide whether visitors may do basic wiping.
2. Clean fallen debris from the debris tray and the machine to reduce fire risk and improve exhaust. {staff_only: true}
3. Regularly remove the cutting grid to clear small pieces that have stuck or fallen through. {staff_only: true}
4. Keep the rails, motors and moving parts free of excess material. {staff_only: true}
5. Lubricate the wheel track and belts with a light layer of synthetic oil or grease. {staff_only: true}
   REVIEW: The source says lubrication may be needed every few days, depending on use.
