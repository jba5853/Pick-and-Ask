# Which materials can I cut?

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 6, Safety During Operation; p. 8, Materials; p. 7, Fire Safety · Retrieved 2026-11-12
> REVIEW: Steel cutting needs a different lens and setup. See the steel focusing doc.

Never cut PVC, vinyl or unknown materials. The manual lists maximum thicknesses for common materials on the 150W machine.

## Steps

1. Never engrave or cut anything containing PVC or vinyl. These materials give off a highly toxic, corrosive vapour.
2. Never engrave or cut an unknown material.
3. Check the manufacturer's MSDS sheet to see how the material reacts to extreme heat. {staff_only: true}
4. Maximum thickness (150W CO2): acrylic 20mm, soft wood 15mm, plywood 12mm, leather 6mm, rubber 6mm.
   REVIEW: For acrylic the source gives 20mm and 25/64" (about 10mm), which conflict. These limits are for the 150W tube; confirm the local machine.
5. Do not stack materials, because stacking increases the risk of flames spreading or the workpiece catching fire. {staff_only: true}
