# Testing laser output power

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 20, Section IV. Tests & Adjustments, Laser Test · Retrieved 2026-11-12

A test fire at high power should read 28-32 mA on the gauge, which confirms the tube is working.

## Steps

1. Turn the ignition key on, switch the on/off switch on, and let the machine boot. {staff_only: true}
2. Turn on the water chiller and clear any air bubbles in the tube. {staff_only: true}
3. Move the laser head to the centre of the work area with no material under it. {staff_only: true}
4. Set Max Power and Min Power for Tube 1 to 98%, and turn the mA knob fully right. {staff_only: true}
5. Press and hold Pulse and read the mA gauge. A reading of 28-32 mA means the laser is firing at full power. {staff_only: true}
   REVIEW: The source says Pulse is on the 'touch panel' here and on the 'control panel' elsewhere.
