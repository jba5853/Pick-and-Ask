# Smoke, fumes or odours not being cleared

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, p. 6, Safety During Operation; pp. 14-15, Connect the Exhaust System; p. 30, General Maintenance · Retrieved 2026-11-12
> REVIEW: The fume extractor is an optional upgrade. Confirm whether this space uses outside venting or an extractor.

The laser must not run without working ventilation. Fume extractor suction drops and odours appear as the filters clog.

## Signs

- Fume extractor suction seems reduced.
- Odours are detected.
- Smoke is not being cleared from the work area.

## Steps

1. Do not run the laser unless the ventilation system is working properly.
2. Check that both exhaust fans are connected and on.
   REVIEW: Visitors should only check that the fans are running. Connections are for staff.
3. Staff: clean the pre-filters often with a shop vacuum and check them weekly. Replace them when about 70% of the debris remains after cleaning. {staff_only: true}
4. Staff: clean the HEPA filters as soon as suction seems reduced. Replace them when cleaning no longer restores suction. {staff_only: true}
5. Staff: clean the activated carbon filters when odours are detected or suction drops. Replace them when cleaning no longer removes the odours. {staff_only: true}
6. Never use the fume extractor without all filters in place. {staff_only: true}
