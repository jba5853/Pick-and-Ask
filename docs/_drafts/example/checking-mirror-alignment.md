# Checking and adjusting mirror alignment

> Source: https://fslaser.com/static/files/resources/manuals/mc-series-co2-flatbed-manual.pdf, pp. 21-23, Mirror Alignment Procedure · Retrieved 2026-11-12
> REVIEW: These steps are heavily shortened from the source. Use the full manual procedure.
> REVIEW: The source notes that screw orientation guidance may vary between lasers.

Low-power test fires onto thermal paper show whether the beam hits the centre of each mirror and the nozzle. Adjust mirrors 2, 4 and 5 as needed.

## Signs

- The laser was moved, its tube was replaced, or the mirrors may be out of alignment.

## Steps

1. Place thermal tape over mirror 5 at the laser head and press the edges to see the mirror port outline. {staff_only: true}
2. Jog the laser head all the way to the left. {staff_only: true}
3. Set Min and Max Power to 2% and turn the mA knob fully left. {staff_only: true}
4. Press Pulse quickly to test fire. If no mark appears, turn the mA knob up slowly between fires, but never so strong that the paper catches fire. {staff_only: true}
5. If the mark is not centred, open the right tube compartment and adjust mirror 2 until it is, then lock the nuts. {staff_only: true}
6. Jog the head all the way to the right and test fire again. {staff_only: true}
7. If the second mark is more than 1-2mm from the first, open the left compartment and adjust mirror 4 until the marks overlap. {staff_only: true}
8. Lower the head with Z-, put thermal paper on the air cone port and test fire. If the dot touches the edge, adjust mirror 5. {staff_only: true}
9. Tighten all lock nuts that were loosened. {staff_only: true}
