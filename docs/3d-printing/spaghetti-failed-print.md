# Spaghetti (failed print)

> Source: https://help.prusa3d.com/article/spaghetti-monster_1999 · Retrieved 2026-11-11

## Signs

A tangled, loose mess of filament on and around the print, like a pile of spaghetti. The printer keeps extruding into the air because the part underneath is no longer where it should be.

## Common causes

- The print came loose from the bed during printing.
- The print collapsed partway through (for example, thin or unsupported parts).
- An error in the 3D model or the sliced file.

## How to fix it

1. Stop the print, let the bed cool, then remove the mess. {staff_only: true}
2. Check the first layer sticks well across the whole sheet. See first-layer-not-sticking.md.
3. Check the model in the slicer's Preview. A warning triangle next to the model flags problems. Repair broken models before printing.
4. Re-run the print with the bed temperature 5–10 °C higher. You don't need to re-slice. On Prusa printers, start the print and, after bed leveling finishes, change it in LCD Menu, Tune, Bed. {staff_only: true}
