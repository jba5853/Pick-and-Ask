# Loading and unloading filament

## Unloading filament

1. On the printer screen, open the Filament menu and choose Unload Filament.
2. Pick the material that is loaded so the nozzle heats to the right temperature, and wait.
3. When the printer has pushed the filament back, pull it out gently. If it is stuck, open the extruder idler lever and try again. {staff_only: true}

## Loading filament

1. Cut the end of the filament at a clean 45° angle.
2. On the printer screen, open the Filament menu and choose Load Filament, then pick the material.
3. Push the filament into the extruder until the gears grab it. Make sure it isn't snagged on the spool or a guide tube.
4. Wait until plastic comes out of the nozzle. When the printer asks whether the color is correct and only the new color is coming out, confirm. If nothing comes out, push the filament gently toward the extruder and choose Retry.

## Switching materials

Load the new filament at the higher of the two temperatures, so it pushes the old material out. For example, after printing PETG or ABS, load PLA at the PETG or ABS temperature.

If loading keeps failing or times out, see filament-jam-and-runout.md.
