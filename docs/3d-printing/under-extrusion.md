# Under-extrusion

> Source: https://help.prusa3d.com/article/under-extrusion_2007 · Retrieved 2026-11-11

## Signs

Not enough plastic comes out. Layers have gaps, thin or missing lines, or a spongy look, and parts are weak or break apart.

## Common causes

- Nozzle set too close to the bed, which blocks flow.
- A partly clogged nozzle.
- Dirty or misaligned extruder gears, or wrong idler tension.
- Hotend not cooled properly (blocked or broken hotend fan), which causes heat creep.
- Wrong temperature for the filament, or damp filament.
- Old filament left in the nozzle after a material change.

## How to fix it

1. Re-run the first layer calibration, especially after the printer was moved, the nozzle was changed or the print sheet was swapped. {staff_only: true}
2. Use the slicer's stock profile for your printer, filament and nozzle size.
3. Check the hotend fan spins and isn't blocked by filament or debris. {staff_only: true}
4. Check the extruder gears for dirt and that the idler is neither too loose nor too tight. {staff_only: true}
5. If the filament isn't in the slicer list, try adjusting the nozzle temperature by 5–15 °C.
6. Keep filament dry, especially PETG, ABS and soluble filament.
7. When switching materials, load the new filament at the higher of the two temperatures so it pushes the old one out (for example, load PLA at the PETG temperature after printing PETG). {staff_only: true}
8. If the nozzle seems blocked, do a cold pull. See clogged-nozzle.md. {staff_only: true}
