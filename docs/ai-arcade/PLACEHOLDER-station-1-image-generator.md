## Station 1: Image Generator

**What it is:** A touchscreen kiosk with a blue "Station 1" sign where you type a short description and an AI tool creates a picture from it.

**How to use it:**
1. Tap "Start" on the touchscreen.
2. Type a description of the picture you want, for example "a lion reading a book in a library".
3. Tap "Create" and wait about 20 seconds.
4. Tap "Try again" to get a different version of the same description.

**Saving your picture:** Tap "Send to email" to receive a copy. Pictures are not stored on the kiosk after you tap "Done".

**Time limit:** Please keep sessions to 10 minutes when others are waiting.
