# Bound to Feel (activity A005)

> Source: https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/a005 · Retrieved 2026-10-28

**Create within limits. Communicate with power.** A video storytelling challenge. You draw a random emotion and a creative constraint (for example, "euphoria without characters"), then use Midjourney's video tools to make a short, expressive scene.

- Time: 15–20 minutes
- Difficulty: Medium
- Tools: Midjourney, and ChatGPT (optional)

## Step 1: Set your creative boundaries

1. Click **Draw Emotion** to reveal the emotion your video should convey.
2. Click **Draw Constraint** to reveal your creative limitation.
3. Try to stick with your first draw. If you're completely stuck, you may redraw once per category.

## Step 2: Imagine the scene

Think about what your emotion and constraint could look like. You can ask ChatGPT for ideas, for example: "Help me write a Midjourney prompt for euphoria using muted color palettes and extreme closeups."

## Step 3: Create with Midjourney

1. Open Midjourney and go to the **Create** page. Write a detailed prompt that matches your Emotion and Constraint cards. If you used ChatGPT, paste its prompt into the text field at the top.
2. Midjourney makes four images. Pick one.
3. Hover over the image and click **Animate**. Midjourney makes 4 short animations.

## Step 4: Extend your video

1. Hover over your finished video in Midjourney.
2. Select **Extend Manual**.
3. Change your prompt slightly.
4. Click the **Submit** (arrow) button.

Repeat to build a short emotional journey of 2–3 evolving clips.
