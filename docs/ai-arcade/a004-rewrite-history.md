# Rewrite History (activity A004)

> Source: https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/a004 · Retrieved 2026-10-28

**Reimagine the past. Rethink the future.** An interactive storytelling activity: choose a major historical event, imagine a different outcome, and turn it into a short video.

- Time: 10–15 minutes
- Difficulty: Medium
- Tools: ChatGPT Pro, Sora, and Suno (optional)

## Part 1: Rewrite the past in ChatGPT

Use the prompts on the activity page, in order:

1. Ask ChatGPT to list 5 major historical events that shaped the world.
2. Ask it for 5 alternate-history "What if…" scenarios for the event you choose.
3. Use the page's alternate history prompt to have ChatGPT write a short story (3–4 paragraphs) about your scenario.

## Part 2: Create your new reality in Sora

1. Use the ChatGPT prompt on the activity page to turn your story into a cinematic Sora video prompt with three scenes.
2. Open Sora and click **Storyboard** in the composer.
3. Click on the timeline to create three storyboard cards.
4. Copy and paste your prompt from ChatGPT into each storyboard scene.
5. Click **Create** to generate your video.

## Optional: add a soundtrack in Suno

1. Click **Create** in the Suno menu.
2. Make sure you are in the **Simple** interface.
3. Select the **Instrumental** option.
4. Copy the soundtrack prompt from the activity page into the prompt field, and edit the bracketed text.
5. Click **Create**.
