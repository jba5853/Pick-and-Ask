# Postcard Portals (activity A006)

> Source: https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/a006 · Retrieved 2026-10-28

**Send a glimpse from another time, place, or world.** Use Midjourney to make a postcard from a magical world, a distant planet, a lost civilization or a favorite historical moment.

- Time: 5–10 minutes
- Difficulty: Medium
- Tool: Midjourney

## Step 1: Choose your destination

Choose a location type, a visual style, a genre or theme, and a mood. Then fill in this template:

"a [location type], in a [genre/theme] style, illustrated with [visual style], featuring [key visual elements], aged paper texture, vintage postcard border, faded details, the mood is [atmosphere] --ar 4:3"

Example: "a floating market above the clouds, in a dreamy magical realism style, illustrated with soft watercolor strokes, featuring boats made of leaves and glowing lanterns, aged paper texture, vintage postcard border, faded ink details, the mood is peaceful and surreal --ar 3:4"

## Step 2: Paint it with words in Midjourney

1. Click **Create** in the Midjourney menu.
2. Select the folder icon.
3. Select the **Postcard Portal** folder.
4. Copy and paste your completed prompt.
5. Click the slider icon to open more image settings, and adjust them to fit your idea.
6. Set the image size to landscape.
7. When your settings look good, select the send icon to submit your prompt.
8. Use Creation Actions to resubmit the prompt or make variations of the images.
9. Use the Animate Image options to make short animations of your image.

## Saving and sharing your postcard

When you love your postcard, select the **download** button, then share it to the AI Arcade's Postcard Portals Padlet (padlet.com/tltlearnfirst/PostcardPortals). Look at other visitors' postcards on the Padlet and post your own before moving on.
