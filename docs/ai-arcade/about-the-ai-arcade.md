# About the AI Arcade

> Source: https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/about/ · Retrieved 2026-10-28

## What it is

The AI Arcade is an in-person space inside the Shields Media Commons, next to the Dreamery. It is open to faculty and students. You can use iPads loaded with premium AI tools (ChatGPT, Midjourney and Suno), custom-designed activities, and retro arcade games reimagined with AI. The AI Arcade is an initiative of Teaching and Learning with Technology (TLT) at Penn State.

## Two ways to visit

- **Self-service:** walk up anytime and explore the iPads, AI tools and games at your own pace.
- **By appointment:** for guided support, schedule a session with a consultant using the Media Commons appointment form. When booking, choose "In-Person" as the appointment style and "Media Commons" as the project category. That unlocks the AI Arcade option.

## Getting help

- Ask a Media Commons staff member, or book an appointment at Media Commons.
- For questions, email aiarcade@psu.edu.

## Saving your work and privacy

- The AI Arcade does not claim ownership of what you create.
- Work made with AI tools and institutional accounts may be subject to platform terms, university policies and changing copyright guidance.
- The AI Arcade may share anonymized examples of participant work, with identifying details removed. It asks your explicit permission before crediting you by name or affiliation.
- The AI Arcade cannot promise to keep your creations. Save any work you want to keep.
- To keep your work private, delete your files, outputs or saved content before you leave, or tell a staff member.
