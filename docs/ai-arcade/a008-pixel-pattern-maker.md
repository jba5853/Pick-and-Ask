# Pixel Pattern Maker (activity A008)

> Source: https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/a008 · Retrieved 2026-10-28

**Have fun in the matrix.** Create and interpret colorful 7×7 pixel patterns on the RGB light wall, and explore how AI and humans see images differently.

- Time: 3–5 minutes
- Difficulty: Easy
- Tools: the Govee light app, and the Pixel Pattern Maker custom GPT in ChatGPT

## Get set up

1. Open the **Govee** RGB light app from the iPad dock. It controls the physical light panels.
2. Select a light panel to work with: left or right.
3. Select the panel's **Finger Sketch** mode.

## Make a pattern

Open the Pixel Pattern Maker custom GPT in a new tab and click one of its mode buttons to create a 7×7 color pattern:

- **Random:** the AI makes a surprise pattern.
- **Suggest:** you tell the AI what to make.
- **Guess:** try to identify a hidden shape.
- **Build:** the AI names an object, and you recreate it.

When your pattern appears, go back to the activity page.

## Recreate your pattern on the light wall

In the Govee app's light grid, set the fill color of each square to match the color of the same square in the 7×7 pattern.

Then look at the wall: can you see a shape the AI didn't describe?
