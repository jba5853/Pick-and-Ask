# Brain Beats (activity A002)

> Source: https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/a002 · Retrieved 2026-10-28

**Turn facts into tracks.** Turn something you're learning into a catchy memory song. Use ChatGPT to write the lyrics and Suno to set them to music.

- Time: 3–5 minutes
- Difficulty: Medium
- Tools: ChatGPT Pro, Suno

## Step 1: Generate your lyrics in ChatGPT

Pick a topic from class or from life, and ask ChatGPT to write a song about it. Examples:

- "Make a pop song about the American Revolution."
- "Write a sea shanty explaining Newton's 3 laws."

To make it better, add a style or emotion (for example, "make it sound like a breakup song") or add constraints (for example, "use only simple words and include the word 'photosynthesis' at least 5 times").

## Step 2: Create your memory track in Suno

1. Click **Create** in the Suno menu.
2. Select the **Brain Beats** workspace.
3. Make sure the **Custom** option is selected.
4. Copy your lyrics from ChatGPT and paste them in.
5. Click **Create** to generate your song.
