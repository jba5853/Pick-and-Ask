# AI Arcade tools

> Sources: https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/tools/, https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/t001, https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/t002, https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/t003 · Retrieved 2026-10-28

The AI Arcade iPads have three premium AI tools.

## ChatGPT Pro

An AI assistant for ideas, answers and writing. ChatGPT Pro gives access to OpenAI's most advanced models. You can upload and analyze images, use voice mode for spoken prompts and playback, and try experimental tools like Sora video generation.

Used in: Brain Beats (writing lyrics), Rewrite History (story and video prompts, plus Sora for the video), Bound to Feel (optional help with prompts), and Pixel Pattern Maker (the Pixel Pattern Maker custom GPT).

## Suno

Turns text into fully produced songs with AI. The AI Arcade has the Premier Plan, which can generate up to 8 minutes of audio and split songs into up to 12 vocal and instrumental stems.

Used in: Score The Moment, Brain Beats, and the optional soundtrack in Rewrite History.

## Midjourney

Generates images from text prompts in a wide range of artistic styles, and can animate them. The AI Arcade has a Pro plan. Remix Mode lets you change an image by editing an earlier prompt.

Used in: Postcard Portals and Bound to Feel.
