# Score The Moment (activity A001)

> Source: https://sites.psu.edu/aiarcade/a001/ · Retrieved 2026-10-28

**Every scene deserves a soundtrack.** Become a music director: create a short musical score that captures the emotion and atmosphere of a scene, using AI-generated music in Suno.

- Time: 3–5 minutes
- Difficulty: Easy
- Tool: Suno

## Step 1: Choose your scene

Pick one of the four scene pictures on the activity page: a group in a sunny field, a foggy night road, a wedding dance, or a robot among ruins.

Describe the scene through music. Think about its mood, genre, instruments and emotion. Example: "A happy country song with rising strings and a soft piano, expressing the feeling of emerging into a brighter future."

Copy your scene description to the clipboard before moving on.

## Step 2: Generate your track in Suno

1. Click **Create** in the Suno menu.
2. Select the **Score The Moment** workspace.
3. Paste your description into the **Describe your song** field.
4. Click **Create** to generate your songs.

Bonus: click a genre to add it to your prompt.
